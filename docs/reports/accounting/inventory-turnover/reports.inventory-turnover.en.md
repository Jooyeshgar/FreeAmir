# Inventory turnover

In **Reports → Accounting → Inventory Turnover**, select a Jalali date range, warehouse, and product group. For products with an inventory account, the report shows opening, incoming, outgoing, and remaining quantities and amounts; a PDF export is also available.

Incoming movements include purchases, sales returns, and voids; outgoing movements include sales and purchase returns. Incoming and outgoing amounts use average cost. Reconcile them against invoices and the [inventory costing guide](../../../warehouse/products/inventory-costing.en.md). The start date cannot follow the end date, and selected warehouses and groups must belong to the active fiscal year.
