# General ledger

In **Reports → Accounting → Ledger**, select a top-level account and filter by date, document number, or title. The report includes transactions for that account and all its descendants, with a running balance. This balance starts at zero with the first matching row; it does not include the balance before the selected range.

The report is limited to the active fiscal year and reads approved and unapproved documents. Use the [trial balance](../trial-balance/reports.trial-balance.en.md) to compare a full balance, and the [accounting reports guide](../documents/accounting-reports.en.md) for export details.
