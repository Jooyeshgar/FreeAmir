# Banks Guide

This guide explains how to create, edit, and delete banks in the current version of FreeAmir.

## Before you start

- Confirm the active fiscal year. Banks are displayed within its scope.
- The menu path is **Management → Finance → Banks**. Both menu visibility and bank operations depend on the user's permissions.

## Create and manage a bank

Open **Management → Finance → Banks** and choose **Create Bank**. A bank name is required, may contain at most 20 characters, and is limited to letters, digits, and spaces. The bank page lets you create, edit, and delete banks.

Before deleting a bank, check its related bank accounts, documents, cheque books, and cheques.
