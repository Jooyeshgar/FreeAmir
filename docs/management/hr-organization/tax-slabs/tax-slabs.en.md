# Yearly Tax Slabs

Path: **Management → HR & Organization → Yearly Tax Slabs**. Review or enter slabs before payroll calculation so the period uses the intended tax basis. The list supports creating, viewing, and editing a slab. The current form takes only an income ceiling (`income_to`) and a tax rate (`tax_rate`): the ceiling can be blank (unlimited), and the rate is required and must be between 0 and 100 percent. The form has no year field. After saving, check the settings and a sample [payroll](../../../hr/payrolls/payrolls.en.md).
