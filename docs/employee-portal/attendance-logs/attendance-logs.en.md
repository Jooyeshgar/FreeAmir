# My Attendance

Open **My Portal → My Attendance** to view your attendance logs. You can filter by Jalali start and end dates and by manual or automatic entry. The table shows entry and exit times, worked minutes, overtime, delay, status, and notes. A linked monthly attendance record is shown when one exists.

This page is read-only and limited to the employee record linked to your account. Ask the attendance administrator to correct an inaccurate entry, then check your [monthly attendance](../monthly-attendances/monthly-attendances.en.md) again.
