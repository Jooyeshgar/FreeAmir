# My Information

Open **My Portal → My Information** to review the identity, organizational, contact, insurance, banking, and employment details in your employee record. **Edit** opens the change-information form. In the current application, that form changes only your first name, last name, account email, and password; ask HR to correct other employee details.

First and last names are required. The email must be valid and unique among users. A password change is optional, but requires at least eight characters and matching confirmation. Review your information page after saving.
