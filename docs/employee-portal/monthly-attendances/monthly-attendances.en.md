# My Monthly Attendance

Open **My Portal → Monthly Attendance**, choose a month, and review work days, presence, absence, and overtime minutes. Use **Details** to open the daily calendar and associated logs for that period.

This page shows only your own monthly records; you cannot edit or recalculate them here. If the figures differ from your [attendance logs](../attendance-logs/attendance-logs.en.md), ask the attendance administrator to review the calculation.
