# My Portal dashboard

Open **My Portal → Dashboard** to see a summary of your employee records. This page shows the five most recent attendance logs, request counts by status, your latest monthly attendance, and your latest payroll. Open the corresponding menu item for details.

Portal access requires a user account linked to an employee record and the email-verification feature to be enabled. The displayed information belongs only to the current employee. If something is missing, ask HR to check your employee-account link and the relevant attendance or payroll data.
