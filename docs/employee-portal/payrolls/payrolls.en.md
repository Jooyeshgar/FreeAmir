# My Payrolls

Open **My Portal → My Payrolls**, choose a month, and review your total earnings, deductions, net payment, and payroll status. **View Detail** opens the items on that payroll.

Only payrolls belonging to your employee record appear here, and you cannot change them. If a month is absent, first ask payroll staff whether its payroll has been created. Report amount discrepancies to payroll staff; changes to [monthly attendance](../monthly-attendances/monthly-attendances.en.md) do not necessarily update an already-created payroll automatically.
