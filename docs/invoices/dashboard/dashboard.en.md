# Invoice Dashboard

Menu path: **Invoices → Invoice Dashboard**. [Open the dashboard in the app]({{ site.app_url }}/invoices/dashboard). Choose start and end dates and select **Apply**; **Reset** restores the default filter.

The cards show product sales revenue, cost of goods sold, gross profit and margin, sales and purchase invoice counts, and sales returns and voids. Product revenue is net of sales returns and VAT; gross profit is revenue minus cost of goods sold. This page is for reviewing trends, not a replacement for reconciling invoices, documents, and accounting reports.

If a figure looks unexpected, check the date range and active fiscal year, then follow the invoice-list link to the underlying records. The [sales invoice guide](../sells/sell/sell.en.md) and [purchase invoice guide](../purchases/buy/buy.en.md) explain entry workflows.
