# Moadian Histories

Menu path: **Invoices → Sales → Moadian Histories**. This page shows invoice submission results for the active company and fiscal year. [Open Moadian Histories in the app]({{ site.app_url }}/invoices/moadian-histories).

## Review a submission

Use the status, invoice-number, or date filter to find a submission. Compare its reference number, UID, and recorded time with the invoice. If the latest entry has a reference number but its status is unknown, select **Check Status**. For the latest failed entry, **Send Again** appears when you have permission. A successful invoice cannot be sent again.

Before your first submission, read [How to use Moadian](how-to-use-moadian.en.md). Company settings require a username, tax ID, certificate upload, and private-key upload.
