# Customer Comments

**[Back to the Customers guide](../README.en.md)**

Customer comments record a rating used to rank customers. You can add a comment from the customer list or an individual customer's page. There is also a separate page listing all comments for one customer.

## Text and rating

Comment text accepts at most 500 characters, and the numeric rating ranges from zero to five. The interface accepts half-star (`0.5`) increments, and its reset button returns the rating to zero. In the add-comment dialog on the customer list, text is required and the 500-character limit is enforced. Entering a rating is optional.

## Create, edit, and delete

You can create, edit, or delete comments from the all-comments page. These operations are controlled by `comments.index`, `comments.create`, `comments.store`, `comments.edit`, `comments.update`, and `comments.destroy` permissions.
